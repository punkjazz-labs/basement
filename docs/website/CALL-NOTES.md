# Website call notes — the designer × the owner

Source: audio transcript of the call (Italian), re-read in full on 2026-08-02.
This file is the durable extraction of everything website-relevant. When a design
round contradicts these notes, the notes win until the designer/the owner revise them.

## The thesis

- **The opportunity is not the software.** "Anyone can build the software."
  What is being sold is the movement and its execution: *"creare la casa per chi
  vuole essere rappresentato da questo tipo di movimento"* — the home for people
  who want to be represented by local AI. The site is that home in every form,
  including how the product is presented.
- **Mini manifesto page.** "Se questo mi diventa una sorta di mini manifesto
  page… sì, c'è anche il software. Però quello che ti sto vendendo non è il
  software."
- **The reference point.** Today you need "100.000 Reddit threads" to understand
  the Spark world. This site must be where everyone doing local AI goes to do
  their thinking *before* buying: "lì sei il mercato delle Spark." Eventually it
  can even sell the Sparks (affiliate links at first).

## Aesthetic direction (the designer's words)

- **Not big tech. Ever.** The first mockup felt "big tech" — that was the
  problem. Crypto-era companies didn't look like big tech; that's the historical
  window again. "Tutto ciò che è corporate looking è l'opposto."
- **Counter-culture, underground, from-the-ground.** Hermes branding is "in
  tono": it doesn't follow standard beauty canons and neither should this.
  OpenClaw doesn't either. The word used: **"Balenciaga"** — ugly-cool, outside
  the canon, deliberate. Also: "lo farei un po' rough."
- **Bold and rough, not polished.** "Un po' più bold. Bold."
- The console/dashboard is exempt: "la dashboard va bene così" — the site is
  where the branding lives, the product stays clean.
- Post-mockup feedback (2026-08-02, after the screen-print full page): "un
  pizzico troppo giocoso… e un po' splashy. Voglio assicurarmi che la gente non
  pensi sia un big tech product that needs to look cool to get your data.
  Comunque mi ha fatto ridere ed ho detto geniale." → dial the hero back a
  little; the anti-big-tech reading must survive.

## Hero content (explicit)

- Use the Spark images: "non so se puoi utilizzare le immagini delle Spark, ma
  sti cazzi io lo farei." (Rights check still pending before launch.)
- Roughly three words: "local AI is finally competitive" / "you can now run
  locally private AI."
- The two DGX machines pictured. Extreme dual-Spark focus: "the best local
  experience you can have today is dual spark." Single Spark supported but the
  flagship story is two. Agentic use only — no image-generation angle.
- Message: **"all you need is two Sparks and this"** — "go buy the Sparks and
  install this thing."

## Required proof artifacts

- **Pareto frontier chart** — the one non-negotiable graphic, ideally visible
  without scrolling. Reference image (from the owner, 2026-08-02): the
  **Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.1** scatter — y = intelligence
  score, x = cost per task in USD on a log scale, so the good corner is
  TOP-LEFT. Dashed crosshair through the best cheap open model; a huge shaded
  box covers everything to its right that scores no higher = the "quadratone"
  ("tutto questo ormai non serve"). Our version must keep those semantics but
  radically simplified (the original plots 128 models). Real data source when
  we build it: Artificial Analysis index + our own benchmark receipts, dated.
- **Electricity calculator** — the calculator the designer described is exactly this:
  "la gente si va a farsi i calcoli di quante elettricità spende se la usa a
  full speed." NOT a payback calculator and NOT an API-price comparison: he
  explicitly concedes cost ("se vuoi usare DeepSeek online ti costa molto di
  meno… lì non competi" — providers have better energy and hardware deals,
  economies of scale). The pitch is local + private at lightbulb prices, not
  cheaper-than-cloud. So: sliders for power/hours/electricity price, outputs in
  absolute dollars per day/month (and tokens produced for scale). Never frame it
  as recouping the hardware.
- **Model curation with receipts.** Speeds and claims only from measured
  community numbers. (Model cards on the site only once real benchmark receipts
  exist.)

## Trust (what the section actually means)

the designer on howtospark-type sites: "Non voglio copiare e incollare i loro comandi
sul mio sito… questo è tutto vibe-coded… non ho la più pallida idea di cosa gira
dentro… ogni tanto mi aggiunge nuove flags… c'è una curation che va migliorata."

→ The trust section's job is to answer *that* fear in plain words: you know
exactly what's running, nothing changes silently, every install is checked and
logged. Jargon like "pinned/receipted" does not communicate this to a visitor.

## Ecosystem ideas (later phases, mentioned on the call)

- **mlx.fast-style community optimization site**: "avremo un mlx.fast simile
  website" — we build the optimization website, everybody participates; a
  one-click "allocate 20% of this machine's time to optimization" toggle.
- Community as talent magnet ("Ethereum è stato questo talent grab").
- NVIDIA narrative surf: position to benefit from the open-source push
  ("io voglio che Jensen domani veda queste cose").
- The lawyer test: adoption must reach the professional who fears cloud data
  ("io ora uso Claude e faccio cose, però ho paura dei dati").
- Full agent (Hermes alternative) explicitly deferred: start as the one-click
  installer + console, not the agent.
